Split Free

Split trips, rent and dinners with friends. Everyone sees the same balances, to the paisa.

Download for Android

Free · No ads · Sign in with Google · Android 8+

Like Splitwise, but free

Add an expense.
Everyone involved gets a ping.

Pay for dinner, split it equally or with exact amounts, and only the people in that expense are notified. Balances update on every phone at once.

Everything it does

Money between friends, sorted

Groups with a view

Name a group after a place and it gets a photo of it. Invite friends by email or a WhatsApp link.

Exact to the paisa

Split equally or unequally, with one payer or several. Totals always add up.

Simplify debts

Combines who-owes-whom so the group settles with the fewest payments.

Instant notifications

New expenses, comments, reminders and invites land on the right phones.

Backed up automatically

A private copy in your own Google Drive's hidden app folder after every change.
